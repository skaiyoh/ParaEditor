Sequence worker

Fetches the models the stages need, then drives the sequence worker over its protocol on the master chosen below: raw RGBA bytes of ?width= x ?height=, and for inpaint the hole as one byte per pixel. ?stages= lists the stages, ?threads= sets the thread count; the report lists every reply. Truth planes, when chosen, are checked against each depth map and plate: near and far, one byte per texel of the depth map, and the ring, one byte per pixel of the master.
